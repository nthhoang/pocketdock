BO THU NHO POCKETDOCK TREN WINDOWS (W1)

1. Giai nen/chep thu muc nay vao mot cho bat ky tren may Windows (vi du Desktop).
2. Bam doi vao file chay-thu.bat va lam theo huong dan tren man hinh.
3. Neu Windows hien canh bao "Windows protected your PC": bam "More info" roi "Run anyway".
   (Do chuong trinh chua co chu ky so, la binh thuong.)
4. Cuoi cung chep thu muc "ketqua" ve.

Chuong trinh khong cai dat gi len may, khong can quyen quan tri, khong can Internet.
Muon don dep: xoa thu muc nay la xong.
